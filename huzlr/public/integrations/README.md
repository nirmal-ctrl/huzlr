# Integration brand assets

These assets are bundled locally to avoid third-party image requests.

- `jira.svg`: unchanged `jira/SVG/Jira_icon.svg` from the [Atlassian logo library](https://atlassian.design/foundations/logos), `jira_app.zip`.
- `linear-dark.svg` and `linear-light.svg`: unchanged `logo-dark.svg` and `logo-light.svg` from the [Linear brand assets](https://linear.app/brand).
- `asana.svg`: complete horizontal logo SVG published in the header of the [Asana brand guidelines](https://asana.com/brand). Keep the symbol and wordmark together, in their original colors, on a white background.

The dark Linear mark is used on light backgrounds; the light mark is used on dark backgrounds. Brand assets remain subject to their owners' trademark guidelines.
